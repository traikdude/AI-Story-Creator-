# The next panel

**Role and audience.** A title-page illustration for readers choosing their next adventure in AI Story Creator. A detective's clue leads the eye across the fold to three possible worlds. The [theme catalog](../../constants.ts) supplies the settings and the [generation prompt](../../services/geminiService.ts) supplies the Western comic style and three-choice motif.

**Primary prompt.** Wide 2:1 landscape full bleed editorial western comic-book print, no text or letters anywhere. Overhead close-up of a vintage open illustrated comic screenplay on cream paper, left page a large atmospheric ink panel showing a lone detective examining a star-shaped clue with a magnifying glass; right page splits into three striking diagonal panels of possible adventures: a dragon circling a tower, a rocket passing a ringed planet, an overgrown jungle arch. Strong charcoal contours, vermilion, teal and marigold flat inks, tactile halftone shadows. Confident graphic composition, edge-to-edge printed illustration, no interface, no lettering.

**Alternate prompt.** Draw a wordless comic cover in four flat spot inks. Enlarge the detective's magnifying lens until a star clue fills its glass. Set three narrow vignettes opposite it: a fantasy keep, a ringed planet and a jungle doorway. Cream gutters separate the scenes. Keep the silhouettes legible at thumbnail size.

**Text in the art.** None. The README carries the title as selectable text.

**Alt text.** An open comic pairs a detective examining a clue with panels of a dragon, a rocket and a jungle arch.

**Placement.** Use [banner.png](banner.png) above the README title. The [social preview](social-preview.png) uses the same 1280 × 640 composition for manual upload. The [editable SVG](banner.svg) uses opaque inks and paper for light and dark backgrounds.

**Refresh** if the story format, choice mechanic or featured themes change. These illustrations represent the premise and are independent of generated in-app scenes.
